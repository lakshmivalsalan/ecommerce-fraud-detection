How it works

  1. CollectTransaction details: signup and purchase time, purchase value, device, IP address, browser, source, age and sex.
  2. Build featuresTime from signup to purchase, a bot-speed flag, accounts sharing the device, and the country of the IP address.
  3. ScoreThe trained XGBoost model gives each account a fraud probability between 0 and 1.
  4. PrioritiseScores of 0.5 or more are High risk, 0.1 to 0.5 Medium, and below 0.1 Low.
